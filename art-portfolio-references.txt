NOTES
================================================================
- Report #1 was the more reliable of the two; Report #2 was heavily
  embellished and its top pick (works-of-arts) could not be verified.
- Many [CHECK] links point to listicles/blogs rather than the template
  itself - follow through to the real demo before committing.
- Recommended build direction (from both reports): a coded Astro or
  Next.js site, Tailwind, headless CMS (Supabase/Sanity/Notion/Prismic),
  masonry or bento gallery, per-artwork detail pages
  (title / year / medium / dimensions / sold status), 5-item nav
  (Home / Gallery / Exhibitions / About / Contact).
