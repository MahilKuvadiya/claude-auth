# Research Brief: Modern Art Portfolio References

> **Purpose:** Hand this document to anyone (a researcher, freelancer, or AI assistant) to find the best modern references for building an artist's portfolio website. The deliverable is a curated, organized list of references — not a finished site.

---

## 1. The Goal

Find the **best modern art portfolio references** for building a website for an **artist who wants to showcase their artwork** (paintings / visual art).

The guiding principle: **let the art fill the screen and keep everything else out of its way.** The strongest artist portfolios are clear, not flashy.

The output of this research will be used by a **developer who will build the site in code**, so references should be useful as *design and structural inspiration* — not necessarily ready-to-ship products.

---

## 2. What Counts as a Reference

Any of the following formats are acceptable. Variety is good — aim for a mix:

- **Live websites** — real artist portfolios that can be visited and studied
- **Figma templates / community files**
- **Coded templates** — Next.js, React, Astro, plain HTML/CSS, Tailwind, etc.
- **Framer / Webflow templates**
- **Design galleries** — Awwwards, Behance, Dribbble, Godly, Land-book entries
- **Any other strong visual reference**

---

## 3. Requirements

**Must have:**
- **Modern (2025–2026 era)** design — current, not dated
- **Art / gallery focus** — built for showcasing visual artwork, NOT a developer/UX résumé portfolio
- **Image-first layouts** — large visuals, generous whitespace, minimal UI clutter
- **Responsive** — works on mobile and desktop

**Nice to have (note where present):**
- Bento-grid or masonry gallery layouts (current 2026 trend)
- Minimal / dark-minimal aesthetic
- Subtle scroll-reveal or hover animations (tasteful, not overwhelming)
- An artwork **detail page** (single piece with title, year, medium, description)
- Sections for: Home / Gallery / Exhibitions / About / Contact
- Optional: ability to sell artwork or take commissions

**Avoid:**
- Generic business/SaaS templates
- Heavy, slow, animation-overloaded sites
- Outdated stock-photo template looks
- Kinetic/animated typography (poor for accessibility, SEO, performance)

---

## 4. Where to Look

- **Figma Community** — sort by *Newest*; search "art portfolio", "art gallery", "bento portfolio"
- **Awwwards** — Sites of the Day in Art / Photography / Portfolio categories
- **Godly** (godly.website), **Land-book**, **Httpster** — curated modern site galleries
- **Behance / Dribbble** — "artist portfolio website" projects
- **Framer & Webflow template marketplaces** — Art / Creative / Portfolio categories
- **GitHub** — open-source portfolio templates (search "artist portfolio", "gallery", framework name)
- **ThemeForest** — art/gallery HTML & Figma templates

---

## 5. Deliverable Format

For **each reference found**, provide a row in this table:

| # | Name | Format (live/Figma/code/Framer…) | Link | Best for | Why it's good | Free or paid |
|---|------|----------------------------------|------|----------|---------------|--------------|

Then add:

- **Top 3 picks** with a 2–3 sentence justification each
- **Common patterns observed** — what the best examples have in common (layout, navigation, typography, color, animation)
- **One recommended direction** — which reference(s) to build from and why

Aim for **12–20 references total**, weighted toward art-specific, modern examples.
